ZenDoku: Sudoku Puzzle Coach

A sudoku app for Android that explains the “why” instead of the answer. No clock, no pressure.

Get it on Google Play →

Sudoku hints that explain the logic

Stuck? Tap the light bulb. ZenDoku shows which cell to look at and explains why that number fits, step by step, so you start spotting the patterns on your own.

Zen mode: no timer, no mistake counter

Play at your own pace with just you and the grid. No clock and no pressure.

What's inside

  • Easy, Medium and Hard sudoku puzzles
  • Pencil notes, undo and redo, optional note auto-fill
  • Daily sudoku challenge: everyone gets the same puzzle each day
  • Streaks, resume unfinished puzzles, review completed ones
  • Korean, English, Japanese, Spanish and Simplified Chinese
Free to play. Ads are shown on some screens, and “Find mistakes” can be used after watching an ad.
