A classic sliding puzzle

Push tiles into the empty space to put them back in order. The old 15 puzzle, rebuilt as a stage-based mobile game.

Features

  • 100 stages across 20 grid sizes, from 3x3 to 7x8
  • Star ratings based on your move count and time
  • Stages unlock in order as you clear them
  • Best records saved automatically
  • Dark mode, sound effects with a mute toggle
  • Korean, English, Chinese, Spanish, French and Japanese, switchable in the app
