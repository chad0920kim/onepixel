AutoPlay: Bluetooth Auto Music

Get in the car or turn on your Bluetooth speaker and your music starts by itself. For Android.

Get it on Google Play →

Auto play music when Bluetooth connects

Pick the Bluetooth devices you want, such as your car stereo, a Bluetooth speaker or earbuds, and the music app to use. When a chosen device connects, AutoPlay opens that app and starts playback.

Pauses when it disconnects

When Bluetooth drops, the music stops. If a call or notification interrupted playback, AutoPlay can resume it for you (this needs notification access).

What's inside

  • Choose which paired devices trigger playback, or react to all of them
  • Works with the music app you pick
  • Set the volume used when playback starts
  • Adjust the start delay and retry interval to suit your music app
  • Interface in Korean, English, Japanese, Spanish and Simplified Chinese

Good to know

  • Music apps handle outside play commands differently, so on some apps or phones auto play may be slow or not work. Increasing the start delay usually helps.
  • To detect connections while the app is closed, battery optimization may need to be turned off for AutoPlay.
